3 · Our test: 35 models, every layer

What is credibility aligned with?

Each line is a concept’s cosine to credibility across depth; bands show the spread over 35 models. At the final layer formality is closest in 29 of 35. Deception and sarcasm are nearly as strong with the opposite sign (sign = label polarity).

4 · The test that can fail

Can angles alone name the concepts?

97%

with shuffled labels or random vectors: chance

Hold out a whole model family. Describe each of its concept vectors only by its angles to the others, then match against the average from the other families.

What it might mean

The consolidation of meaning

Thirty-five models, built by different teams on different text, agree on how their concepts sit relative to one another. Each model’s coordinates are its own; the angles between its concepts are not.

Is this the structure of language itself, or the structure of the world it describes?
