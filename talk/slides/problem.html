Problem

Do different models share one map of concepts?

Every model stores concepts in its own private coordinates, so you cannot lay one model’s directions over another’s.

But the relationships between concepts could still match: is credibility close to formality and opposed to sarcasm in every model?

Why it matters

If the map is shared, what we learn inside one model can be checked in others.

And it tells us whether models learn something about the world, or just quirks of their training.
